How to join with the QR code
since you're likely looking at this on the same phone you'll use to join, here's the easiest way — no second device needed
1
📸Screenshot the QR code
Take a screenshot of the code above (or press and hold the image and save it) so it's sitting in your Photos.
2
💬Open WhatsApp
Go to the Chats tab.
3
📷Tap the camera / QR icon
Top of the Chats tab (or Settings → QR Code) — this opens WhatsApp's built-in scanner.
4
🖼️Choose from your photos
Inside the scanner, tap the small photo/gallery icon and select the screenshot you just saved — WhatsApp reads QR codes from photos, so you don't need to point your camera at your own screen.
5
✅Tap "Join Community"
A preview of Amigas en Seattle will pop up. Tap join, and you're in — say hi!
